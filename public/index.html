VD Project

Vortex Dome · Research & development

The Vortex Dome

Our long-running effort to advance storm chasing technology and safety — purpose-built hardware, live data collection, and real-time monitoring from inside the storm.

Project Focus

  1. Advanced Storm TrackingSystems that keep us positioned on the storm, not chasing behind it.
  2. Weather Data CollectionField measurements gathered where fixed stations can't reach.
  3. Chaser Safety InnovationsHardening the platform so the team can work closer, safely.
  4. Real-Time MonitoringLive telemetry streamed back while the storm is still on the ground.
